Earnings Call Transcript Analysis

I collected earnings call transcripts from The Motley Fool and organized them into two tables, one for full calls and one for individual speaker turns. Each turn was labeled as part of the prepared remarks or the Q&A.

The next stage, Earnings Call Sentence Classifier, uses a Naive Bayes model to classify new sentences as prepared remarks or Q&A responses. The Q&A class uses answers only.
